The practice

Thirty years of dermatology, and a second discipline built on top of it — the aesthetics of the same skin.

Dr. Mona Ibrahim Sabaa qualified in medicine and general surgery at Al Mansoura University in 1993, and returned to formal study fifteen years later for a master's in dermatology and andrology at Kasr Al Ainy.

That order matters. The medical training came first and the cosmetic work sits on it — disease of the skin is diagnosed before it is treated, and treated before it is refined. Her clinics carry a full dermatological practice alongside the injectables and energy devices, rather than the aesthetic menu alone.

She is a key opinion leader and injector for Allergan, Galderma, Nauvea, Merz and Promoitalia, and holds the Nice Sophia Antipolis diploma in injection technique and aesthetic volumetry — the study of how the face loses volume, and where it should be returned.

A laser is chosen for the number it emits at, because that number decides what in the skin absorbs it — water, melanin, or haemoglobin. Here is the clinic's full range, placed on the real spectrum: from a 308 nm excimer lamp in the ultraviolet to a fractional CO₂ beam at 10,600 nm, well into the mid-infrared.
